About

{{ site_name }} lists {{ "{:,}".format(total) }} English headwords. Each one shows the British and American pronunciation in the International Phonetic Alphabet, a definition per sense, and example sentences.

Where the data comes from

The entries are built from English Wiktionary, parsed with wiktextract and filtered down to the words a general-purpose dictionary would actually list. Pronunciations keep Wiktionary's own accent labels: Received Pronunciation and its neighbours become the UK transcription, General American and its neighbours the US one.

A small set of hand-written entries overrides the generated ones where the automatic parse was thin — mostly words whose UK/US difference is worth a note.

Limitations

  • One transcription per accent. Real dictionaries list variants; the note field is the escape hatch for the important ones.
  • Not every word has both a UK and a US transcription — Wiktionary's coverage is uneven, and an untagged pronunciation is used for both.
  • IPA rendering depends on your fonts. Rarer symbols (t̬, ɚ) can look uneven in a system font.
  • No audio, by design.
