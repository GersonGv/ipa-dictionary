        Reading the notation

        Symbols are written between slashes. A few that trip people up:

        ˈ
        Main stress — the syllable right after it is the strong one.
        ˌ
        Secondary stress, weaker than the main one.
        ə
        Schwa, the unstressed “uh” in about.
        ː
        The vowel before it is long.
        ɚ / ɝː
        An r-coloured vowel, typical of American English.
        t̬
        A tapped /t/, the quick d-like sound in American better.
